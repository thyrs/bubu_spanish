Common Regular Verbs in Present Tense


1. Verb Conjugation Basics
Spanish verbs are conjugated based on the subject (who is performing the action). Regular verbs follow predictable patterns depending on their ending: -ar, -er, or -ir. Here's how these verbs are conjugated in the present tense:

**-ar Verbs**
These verbs follow the present tense endings: -o, -as, -a, -amos, -an.
Amar (to love)

Yo amo (I love)
Usted ama (You love, formal)
Él/Ella ama (He/She loves)
Nosotros amamos (We love)
Ustedes aman (You all love)
Ellos/Ellas aman (They love)


**-er Verbs**
These verbs follow the present tense endings: -o, -es, -e, -emos, -en.

Querer (to want):

Yo quiero (I want)
Usted quiere
Él/Ella quiere
Nosotros queremos
Ustedes quieren
Ellos/Ellas quieren


**-ir Verbs**
These verbs follow the present tense endings: -o, -es, -e, -imos, -en.

Mentir (to lie):

Yo miento (I lie)
Usted miente
Él/Ella miente
Nosotros mentimos
Ustedes mienten
Ellos/Ellas mienten


Example Sentences
Here are some simple sentences to see these verbs in action:

Comer: Yo **como** pizza los viernes. (I eat pizza on Fridays.)
Querer: Usted **quiere** un café. (You want a coffee.)
Vivir: Nosotros **vivimos** en una casa grande. (We live in a big house.)
Mentir: Él **miente** sobre su edad. (He lies about his age.)
