# 📝 Lesson: Possessives (Los Posesivos)

---

## 🗣️ Examples in Sentences

```
Mi hermano es alto
mi er-ma-no es al-to

```

```
Su gato es negro
su ga-to es ne-gro
His / Her / Their cat is black
```

```
Nuestra familia es grande
nues-tra fa-mi-lia es gran-de
Our family is big
```

```
Sus amigos son divertidos
sus a-mi-gos son di-ver-ti-dos
Their friends are fun
```

---

## 🔔 Important Rules to Remember!

> ✅ The possessive **must match the noun** — not the owner!
> - **Nuestro** → used with **masculine** nouns → *nuestro perro*
> - **Nuestra** → used with **feminine** nouns → *nuestra gata*
> - **Mi / Su** → same for both genders → *mi casa, mi perro*
