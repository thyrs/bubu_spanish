# 🔤 Spanish Pronunciation: The Letter **J**

---

## Why This Matters for You

In Spanish, **J makes ONE sound only.**
It never changes. It is always the same.
This is **easier than English!**

---

## The Sound of J in Spanish

**J = a strong H sound, like breathing out hard**

Like when you breathe on your glasses to clean them.
Or like the English word **"hot"** — but stronger, from your throat.

> 🇿🇲 Like the **"h"** in Chewa words — a strong breath sound.

---

## 👂 Listen to the Pattern

```
ja = ha
je = he
ji = hi
jo = ho
ju = hu
```

## 🚨 The Tricky Part — G Also Makes J Sound!

In Spanish, the letter **G before E or I** makes the **same sound as J**.

```
| Word       | Pronunciation   | Meaning     |
| ---------- | --------------- | ----------- |
| gente      | **hen-te**      | people      |
| general    | **he-ne-ral**   | general     |
| elegante   | **e-le-gan-te** | elegant     |
| agente     | **a-hen-te**    | agent       |
```

> ✅ Rule: **G + A, O, U** = hard G (like English "go")
> ✅ Rule: **G + E, I** = sounds like J = **H sound**

## ✅ Remember — The Rule is Simple!

> **J in Spanish = always "H" sound**
> **Strong breath from your throat**
> **It NEVER sounds like English J (like "jungle")**
