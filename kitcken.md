## 💬 Kitchen Phrases:
```
| English                | Spanish                | Pronunciation                |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| I am hungry            | Tengo hambre           | **ten-go am-bre**            |
| Where is the...?       | ¿Dónde está el...?     | **don-de es-ta el**          |
| I am going to cook     | Voy a cocinar          | **boy a ko-si-nar**          |
| Can I use the kitchen? | ¿Puedo usar la cocina? | **pwe-do u-sar la ko-si-na** |
```
