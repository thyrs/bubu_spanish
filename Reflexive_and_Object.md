Reflexive and Object Pronouns
**Object Pronouns (Direct/Indirect):**
- Me (me): *Ella me ve.* (She sees me.) 
 *Me dio un regalo.* (She gave me a gift.)
- Le (you, formal, indirect): *Le escribí una carta.* (I wrote you a letter.)
- Lo/La (him, her, it, you formal, direct): *Lo invité a la fiesta.* (I invited him to the party.) 
*La vi ayer.* (I saw her yesterday.)
*Lo respeto, usted.* (I respect you.)
- Nos (us): *Nos ayudaron.* (They helped us.)
- Los/Las (them, you all): *Los vi en el parque.* (I saw them in the park.) / *Las llamé.* (I called them [fem.].)  
*Los invito, ustedes.* (I invite you all.)

**Reflexive Pronouns:**
- Me (myself): *Me lavo las manos.* (I wash my hands.)
- Se (yourself, formal): *¿Se siente bien, usted?* (Do you feel good?)
- Se (himself, herself, itself): *Él se levanta temprano.* (He gets up early.) / *Ella se peina.* (She brushes her hair.)
- Nos (ourselves): *Nos preparamos para la reunión.* (We prepare ourselves for the meeting.)
- Se (themselves, yourselves): *Ellos se divierten.* (They enjoy themselves.) / *¿Se acuerdan, ustedes?* (Do you all remember?)
