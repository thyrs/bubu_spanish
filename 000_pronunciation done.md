# /s/ vs /k/ sounds
The sound of the letter "c" changes based to the following volew:
Ca - /ka/
Ce - /se/
Ci - /si/
Co - /ko/
Cu - /ku/

Co-ci-na /ko-si-na/ means kitchen
Casa /ka-sa/ means house
Cebo /se-bo/ means fat

# The combination "QU" and the letter "k" always share the same sound /k/  
- Queso /ke-so/ (cheese)  
- Queque /ke-ke/ (cake)  
- Kilo /ki-lo/ (kilogram)  
- Quico /ki-ko/ (nickname)

# S and Z
The letter "S" and "Z" share always the same sound /s/

Sábado /sá-ba-do/ (Saturday)
Zacate /sa-ka-te/ (grass,)
Sapo /sa-po/ (toad)
Zarza /sar-sa/ (blackberry bush)

# B vs V  
The sound of these 2 letters is always the same: /b/  
- Velo /be-lo/ (veil, speed)  
- Bola /bo-la/ (ball)  
- Baba /ba-ba/ (drool, slime)  
- Vena /be-na/ (vein)

# The CH combination  
The "ch" has its own sounds the same as in English
- Choco /cho-ko/ (chocolate,)  
- Chun-che /chun-che/ (thing)  
- Chan-cho /chan-cho/ (pig)

# The "LL" and "Y" share the same sound in the beginning or middle of a word  
- Llano /ya-no/ (plain)  
- Llave /ya-ve/ (key)  
- Yoyo /yo-yo/ (yoyo)  
- Tocayo /to-ka-yo/ (namesake)  
- Soya /so-ya/ (soy)
* doble "L" > "LL" sounds like "Y" in spanish
* simple "L" and Double "LL" have NOT the same sound in spanish
* If the letter "Y" is at the end of a word, it sounds like /i/  
- Voy /boi/ (I go)  
- Soy /soi/ (I am)
- Doy /doi/ (i give)

# The letter H is silent in Spanish  
- Hola /o-la/ (hello)  
- Hi-zo /i-so/ (he/she made,)  
- Hay /ai/ (there is/are)  
- Al-co-hol /al-ko-ol/ (alcohol)



# The X is often pronounced as /ks/ (Same as English)  
- Examen /ek-sa-men/ (exam)  
- Exceso /ek-se-so/ (excess)  
- Sexo /se-kso/ (sex)

# The Ñ has its own sound /ñ/  
- Niño /ni-ño/ (boy)  
- Caño /ka-ño/ (pipe)  
- Leño /le-ño/ (firewood)

# The W has the same sound as in English  
- Walter /wal-ter/ (name)  
- Waco /wa-co/ (name)

The combination "gü" also sounds like "W "

* Vergüenza. /berwensa/ (shame)
* Pingüino. /pinwino/ (pinguin)
* Bilingüe. /bilinwe/ (Bilingual)
