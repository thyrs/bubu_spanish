# 🔤 Lesson: Common Regular Verbs in Present Tense

---

## 📌 1. Verb Conjugation Basics

In Spanish, verbs change their endings depending on **who** is doing the action.
Regular verbs follow predictable patterns based on their ending: **-ar**, **-er**, or **-ir**

> 💪 **This is essential! You need verbs to speak. Learn these and you can work, communicate and survive in Costa Rica!**

---

## 🔵 -AR Verbs

> Endings: **-o, -as, -a, -amos, -an**

### Example: **Amar** (to love)

```
Yo amo
jjo a-mo
I love
```

```
Usted ama
u-sted a-ma
You love (formal)
```

```
Él / Ella ama
el / e-jja a-ma
He / She loves
```

```
Nosotros amamos
no-so-tros a-ma-mos
We love
```

```
Ustedes aman
u-ste-des a-man
You all love
```

```
Ellos / Ellas aman
e-jjos / e-jjas a-man
They love
```

## 🌟 Bonus - Verbs You Will Use at Work in Costa Rica!

> 🇨🇷 These are the most important verbs for working in Costa Rica!

| Verb         | Meaning        | Why you need it                       |
| ------------ | -------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| **Trabajar** | To work        | You will say this every day!          |
| **Hablar**   | To speak       | Talk to your boss and customers       |
| **Ayudar**   | To help        | Helping coworkers and clients         |
| **Escuchar** | To listen      | Understanding instructions            |
| **Aprender** | To learn       | You are doing it right now! 💪       |
| **Vender**   | To sell        | Common in markets and shops           |
| **Recibir**  | To receive     | Receiving orders and payments         |
| **Subir**    | To go up/climb | Very useful on farms and construction |
