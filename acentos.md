# 🔤 Lesson: Accents in Spanish (Los Acentos)

---

## 📌 What is an Accent?

> Every Spanish word has **one syllable that sounds stronger/harder** than the others.
> This is called the **stress**.

---

## 📌 What is a Syllable?

> Syllables are small chunks of a word — usually **2-3 letters** combined.

```
casa
ka-sa
house
```

> **ca** = syllable 1 from the right
> **sa** = syllable 2 from the right

> 🔔 **Important:** We **count syllables from right to left** in Spanish!

---

## 📌 What is a Tilde?

> A **tilde ( ´ )** is the little mark written above a vowel:
> **á, é, í, ó, ú**
> It tells you → **stress THIS syllable!**

> 🔔 The tilde is **only placed on vowels**, never on consonants!

---

## 📋 The 3 Accent Rules

---

## 🔵 Rule 1 — Stress on the LAST syllable

> The stress falls on the **last syllable**

### ✅ When do they get a tilde?
> They **get a tilde** if they end in: **a vowel, n, or s**
> They **do NOT get a tilde** if they end in any other consonant (like r, d, l...)

---

```
sofá
so-FA
sofa — ends in vowel → gets tilde ✅
```

```
canción
kan-SION
song — ends in n → gets tilde ✅
```

```
comer
ko-MER
to eat — ends in r → NO tilde ❌
```

> 💡 **Tip:** Think of these words as words that **"peak" at the end!**

---

## 🟢 Rule 2 — Stress on the SECOND-TO-LAST syllable

> The stress falls on the **second syllable from the right**
> 🔔 This is the **most common pattern in Spanish** — the default!

### ✅ When do they get a tilde?
> They **get a tilde** if they do NOT end in: **a vowel, n, or s**
> They **do NOT get a tilde** if they end in a vowel, n, or s

---

```
árbol
AR-bol
tree — ends in l → gets tilde ✅
```

```
lápiz
LA-pis
pencil — ends in z → gets tilde ✅
```

```
casa
KA-sa
house — ends in vowel → NO tilde ❌
```

```
sol
SOL
sun — ends in l → gets tilde ✅
```

> 💡 **Tip:** This is the **default setting** of Spanish — most words follow this rule!

---

## 🟠 Rule 3 — Stress on the THIRD-TO-LAST syllable

> The stress falls on the **third syllable from the right**
> These are less common but easy to spot!

### ✅ When do they get a tilde?
> **ALWAYS** — no exceptions! Every word in this group has a tilde!

---

```
música
MU-si-ka
music — stress on 3rd syllable → ALWAYS gets tilde ✅
```

```
teléfono
te-LE-fo-no
telephone — stress on 3rd syllable → ALWAYS gets tilde ✅
```

> 💡 **Tip:** If you see a tilde on the **third-to-last syllable** — you know immediately which syllable to stress!

---

## 📋 Quick Summary Table


###  **Rule 1** 
- Last syllable        
- Ends in vowel, n, or s         
- sofá, canción 

###  **Rule 2** 
- 2nd-to-last syllable 
- Does NOT end in vowel, n, or s 
- árbol, lápiz  

###  **Rule 3** 
- 3rd-to-last syllable 
- ALWAYS                         
- música, teléfono

## ✍️ Exercise 1 - Which syllable is stressed?

Say these words out loud and mark the stressed syllable!

**1.** ca-sa →
**2.** so-fá →
**3.** mú-si-ca →
**4.** co-mer →
**5.** te-lé-fo-no →

---



1. **CA**-sa → Rule 2 (second-to-last)
2. so-**FÁ** → Rule 1 (last syllable)
3. **MÚ**-si-ca → Rule 3 (third-to-last)
4. co-**MER** → Rule 1 (last, no tilde — ends in r)
5. te-**LÉ**-fo-no → Rule 3 (third-to-last)

## ✍️ Exercise 2 - Does it need a tilde?

Write **YES** (needs tilde) or **NO** (no tilde needed)

**1.** comer — stress on last syllable, ends in **r** →
**2.** arbol — stress on second-to-last, ends in **l** →
**3.** casa — stress on second-to-last, ends in **a** →
**4.** cancion — stress on last syllable, ends in **n** →
**5.** musica — stress on third-to-last syllable →

---



1. **NO** → ends in r, Rule 1 = no tilde ❌
2. **YES** → ends in l, Rule 2 = needs tilde → **árbol** ✅
3. **NO** → ends in vowel, Rule 2 = no tilde ❌
4. **YES** → ends in n, Rule 1 = needs tilde → **canción** ✅
5. **YES** → Rule 3 = ALWAYS tilde → **música** ✅


---

## ✍️ Exercise 3 - Which rule is it?

Write **Rule 1, Rule 2 or Rule 3**

**1.** sofá →
**2.** casa →
**3.** música →
**4.** árbol →
**5.** canción →

---



1. **Rule 1** → stress on last syllable
2. **Rule 2** → stress on second-to-last (most common!)
3. **Rule 3** → stress on third-to-last
4. **Rule 2** → stress on second-to-last
5. **Rule 1** → stress on last syllable
